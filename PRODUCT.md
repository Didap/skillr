# PRODUCT.md — Helllo

## Product Purpose
Helllo (formerly Skillr) is the "Tinder for work": a rapid, double-opt-in matching platform between professionals and companies. It eliminates the friction of traditional job boards by focusing on total transparency and zero-time-wasted interactions.

## Users
1. **Professionals**: Students, freelancers, and full-time workers looking for their next gig or career move. They value speed, rate transparency, and verified company seriousness.
2. **Companies**: Startups and established firms looking for talent (React devs, designers, PMs, etc.). They value VAT-verified partners, a direct line to their matches and quick call booking.

## Brand Tone
- **Direct & Transparent**: No "corporate speak." Clear rates, clear expectations.
- **Dynamic & Fluid**: Fast-paced, interaction-heavy (swipe), mobile-first.
- **Warm & Cheeky**: Every match is a "hello", an icebreaker rather than an application. Friendly, never childish; it is still a tool for serious connections.
- **Helllo Palette**: viola, rosso, rosa, blu and crema in flat blocks (see DESIGN.md).

## Strategic Principles
1. **Total Transparency**: Salary/Rate is always visible and mandatory.
2. **Chat after the match, then the call**: Only mutual matches can write to each other (no cold messages). The chat exists to break the ice and get to the 30-minute call.
3. **Double Opt-in**: Safety and mutual interest by default.
4. **Verified Trust**: VAT verification for companies is mandatory.
